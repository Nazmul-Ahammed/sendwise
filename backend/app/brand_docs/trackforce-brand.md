## 01 Ideal Customer Profile

TrackForce is designed for organizations that need clearer, faster and more objective insight into how work happens across on-site, remote, hybrid, field and project-based teams.

| **Question**                           | **Answer**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Who Are They?**                      | Business owners, CXOs, HR leaders, Admin teams, Operations Heads, Project Managers, Team Leads, IT/Admin teams, and organizations managing on-site, remote, hybrid, field or project-based employees.                                                                                                      |
| **What Do They Currently Think?**      | Employee productivity can be managed through manual reports, supervisor follow-ups, attendance sheets, task updates and trust-based status reporting. Some may associate workforce software with surveillance rather than business improvement.                                                            |
| **What Problem Do They Face?**         | They lack timely visibility into active time, break patterns, app and website usage, screen evidence, file movement, workload, project progress and productivity trends. This creates weak accountability, delayed reporting, uneven workloads and difficulty managing distributed teams.                  |
| **What Do We Want Them To Think?**     | TrackForce is an intelligent workforce productivity and performance platform that turns daily work activity into clear, actionable insight for better decisions, accountability and operational improvement.                                                                                               |
| **What Action Do We Want?**            | Book a demo, review current workforce visibility gaps, test TrackForce with a team or department, and evaluate the dashboard, automated reports and AI insights.                                                                                                                                           |
| **What Proof Will Convince Them?**     | Real-time dashboard, active and break-time insight, screenshots and screen records, app and website usage, keystroke signals, file-transfer visibility, meeting records, project/task insight, AI summaries, workload reports, risk-user detection, role-based access and automated daily/monthly reports. |
| **What Tone Should We Use?**           | Smart, professional, transparent, productivity-focused, data-driven and trust-building. Focus on performance improvement, operational clarity and accountability rather than fear-based oversight.                                                                                                         |
| **Which Channel Will Influence Them?** | LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube explainers, productivity webinars, HR and operations communities, direct sales meetings, demo sessions, case studies, email campaigns, retargeting ads and CXO-focused thought leadership.                                                                                     |

### Implementation Journey

- Discovery: define business goals, departments, work models, reporting gaps, data sensitivity and success measures.

- Policy design: decide which data is necessary, who can access it, retention period, employee notice and privacy safeguards.

- Pilot scope: select one team or department and define baseline productivity/reporting metrics.

- Configuration: create organization hierarchy, roles, devices, departments, work schedules, productivity categories and policy rules.

- Deployment: onboard users through the approved installation mode and verify data quality.

- Training: train HR, Operations, Team Leads and IT separately according to access level and use case.

- Review: compare pre-pilot and post-pilot reporting time, workload visibility, attendance accuracy and management decision speed.

- Scale: expand to additional teams, connect integrations and formalise automated reporting and governance.
