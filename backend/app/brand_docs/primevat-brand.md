## 01 Ideal Customer Profile

PrimeVAT primarily serves Bangladeshi organisations that need to replace fragmented VAT records, manual calculation and return-cycle pressure with one structured, NBR-enlisted VAT management platform.

| **Question**                            | **Answer**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Who Are They?**                       | Managing Directors, Business Owners, CFOs, Heads of Finance, VAT Managers, Tax Leads, Accounts Teams, Compliance Teams, IT Teams, and Branch/Operations Managers of Bangladeshi businesses. Best-fit industries include manufacturing, trading, retail and wholesale, FMCG, RMG, healthcare, telecom, e-commerce, service businesses, and multi-branch organisations. |
| **What Do They Currently Think?**       | VAT work can be managed through Excel, manual records, accounting software, or external VAT consultants. They may think VAT software is only needed during return submission, not for daily sales, purchase, inventory, branch, rebate, and audit-data management.                                                                                                    |
| **What Problem Do They Face?**          | VAT data is scattered across invoices, purchase records, sales records, branches, inventory, Excel sheets, paper files, and accounting systems. This creates human error, time-consuming return preparation, VAT calculation complexity, missing reports, weak audit readiness, data insecurity, and multi-branch visibility issues.                                  |
| **What Do We Want Them To Think?**      | PrimeVAT is not just VAT entry software. It is an NBR-enlisted VAT management system that helps businesses organise Mushak forms, VAT records, reports, return preparation, multi-branch VAT activity, and audit-ready documentation in one structured platform.                                                                                                      |
| **What Action Do We Want?**             | Book a demo, schedule a VAT process consultation, review the current VAT workflow, and explore how PrimeVAT can organise VAT records before the next return cycle.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **What Proof Will Convince Them?**      | NBR-enlisted software provider status, VAT and SD Act 2012 compliance, Mushak module coverage, multi-branch VAT management, cloud backup, secure server, bulk Excel upload, detailed VAT reports, audit and MIS reports, rapid implementation, and public client references.                                                                                          |
| **What Tone Should We Use?**            | Compliance-focused, serious, practical, trust-building, finance-friendly, and risk-aware. Communication should speak to business control, audit readiness, regulatory confidence, and management visibility.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Which Channels Will Influence Them?** | LinkedIn, Facebook, CFO and Finance communities, VAT and Tax professional networks, YouTube explainers, webinars, sales decks, case studies, email campaigns, direct CFO/VAT Manager outreach, retargeting ads, and industry-specific seminars.                                                                                                                       |

### 3.2 Business Problems PrimeVAT Solves

| **Problem**                             | **Without a VAT Platform**                                                                 | **PrimeVAT Response**                                                                            |
|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Scattered VAT data**                  | Invoices, sales, purchase, inventory, branches and accounting data stay in separate files. | Central VAT records and structured operational modules create one working source for compliance. |
| **Manual Mushak preparation**           | Teams rebuild registers and challans from Excel every return cycle.                        | System-generated NBR-prescribed Mushak reports reduce repeated manual preparation.               |
| **VAT calculation errors**              | Rates, adjustments, rebates and source deductions are handled manually.                    | Automated calculation and configurable tax rules improve consistency and reduce entry error.     |
| **Return-cycle pressure**               | Teams spend days correcting data before Mushak 9.1 preparation.                            | Daily transaction capture and structured reports improve return readiness.                       |
| **Weak audit readiness**                | Documents, challans and adjustments are difficult to trace during audit.                   | Audit-ready records, detailed reports and transaction trails support faster response.            |
| **Multi-branch visibility gap**         | Head office cannot see branch-wise VAT position and stock movement clearly.                | Centralised branch records, stock-transfer documentation and dashboard visibility.               |
| **Inventory and production disconnect** | Raw material, input-output ratio, production and finished goods records do not align.      | Production, purchase, inventory and Input-Output Coefficient workflows stay connected.           |
| **Data security risk**                  | Sensitive VAT records depend on local files, individual employees and irregular backup.    | Secure server, access control and regular cloud backup improve continuity and control.           |

### 3.4 Mushak and Compliance Coverage

| **Mushak 6.2**   | Sales Register                               | Maintains sales transactions and output VAT records for relevant businesses.                                                                                                                                                 |

| **Mushak 6.3**   | VAT Sales Challan                            | Generates VAT-compliant sales challan/invoice with transaction and tax details.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Mushak 6.4**   | Contract Manufacturing                       | Supports applicable contract-manufacturing documentation and records.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Mushak 6.5**   | Stock / Branch Transfer                      | Documents transfer of goods between premises, branches or warehouses.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Mushak 6.6**   | Source Deduction Certificate                 | Supports VDS/source deduction certificate generation and tracking.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Mushak 6.7**   | Credit Note                                  | Records and reports sales reduction or return-related credit adjustments.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Mushak 6.8**   | Debit Note                                   | Records and reports purchase return or increase-related debit adjustments.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Mushak 6.9**   | Turnover Tax Challan                         | Supports turnover-tax challan requirements for applicable entities.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Mushak 6.10**  | Invoice Above Prescribed Threshold           | Maintains prescribed information for invoices exceeding the regulatory threshold.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Mushak 9.1**   | Monthly VAT Return Preparation               | Organises transaction data, liabilities, adjustments, rebates and supporting records for return submission.                                                                                                                  |
