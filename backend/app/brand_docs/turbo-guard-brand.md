## AI and Team Non Negotiables

- Use the name **Turbo Guard** with a space and title case.
- Describe Turbo Guard as a **WordPress security and malware recovery plugin**. Do not imply it protects non-WordPress systems.
- Lead with the user outcome, not a list of security acronyms.
- Do not claim perfect security, guaranteed prevention, zero risk, zero slowdown, or 100 percent malware detection.
- Never publish “the only WordPress security plugin” unless a claim is narrowly defined, independently rechecked, and approved by legal or leadership.
- Do not say all processing is offline. Some opt-in features connect to WordPress.org, WPScan, ipapi.co, OpenAI, and Google APIs.
- Do not say no data ever leaves the server. The accurate claim is that website files are not sent to a vendor scanning cloud; specific metadata is transmitted only for documented, opt-in services.
- Keep Free and Pro boundaries explicit. Free detection and protection are broad; free cleanup is limited to three combined items.
- Treat competitor price, install count, free-tier limit, and feature availability as time-sensitive.
- Do not invent reviews, testimonials, recovery rates, scan benchmarks, customer logos, or savings.

# 01 Executive Brand Summary

Turbo Guard is a freemium WordPress security and malware recovery plugin for site owners, agencies, freelancers, and small teams. It scans site files and the WordPress database, detects malware and SEO spam, checks core-file integrity, protects login and application surfaces, and provides a controlled cleanup path with a backup before deletion. Pro adds unlimited cleanup, Live Traffic, AI Security Advisor, Geo-Fence, scheduled vulnerability scans, detailed vulnerability information, file inspection, and expanded bot protection.[^1]

The product is strongest when a user has an urgent business problem rather than an abstract interest in security: Japanese or pharma spam appearing in Google, a hacked site, suspicious files, a hosting warning, unexplained redirects, or uncertainty after a scanner reports technical findings. Turbo Guard connects four jobs that competitors often market separately: **detect the compromise, clean the site, clean the search footprint, and explain the next action.**

# 08 Product Architecture

## Detect

- Full-site scanning of PHP, JavaScript, and HTML in core, content, and root paths.
- More than 30 documented malware patterns including obfuscation, common web shells, hidden iframes, malicious redirects, and spam payloads.
- Core-file integrity checking against official WordPress.org checksums.
- Database checks across posts, options, and rogue administrator accounts.
- Detection of PHP in uploads and core asset directories.
- Polyglot image backdoor detection.
- Japanese, Chinese, and Korean SEO-spam text detection.
- Vulnerability checking for WordPress core, plugins, and themes through WPScan data.

## Protect

- Application firewall controls for SQL injection, cross-site scripting, directory traversal, and PHP uploads.
- IP blocking through exact address, CIDR, range, and wildcard patterns.
- Request rate limiting.
- Brute-force protection, login logs, new-IP admin alerts, and automatic attacker blocking.
- TOTP two-factor authentication with recovery codes.
- Security headers, WordPress version hiding, user-enumeration blocking, and optional XML-RPC, REST API, and file-editor restrictions.
- Bad-bot controls for common scanners and scrapers.
- File watcher with periodic change detection.

## Clean and Recover

- Selection of critical threats for bulk action.
- Automatic ZIP backup before deletion.
- Quarantine into a protected directory.
- Free cleanup of up to three combined items.
- Pro unlimited malware and SEO-spam cleanup.
- Google Search Console connection for indexed-spam discovery, removal requests, and sitemap resubmission.

## Explain and Monitor

- Severity, path, threat name, and file-size context in findings.
- Pro file-content view before cleanup.
- Pro AI Security Advisor with attack-campaign classification and numbered next steps.
- Pro email advisory after scans.
- Pro Live Traffic with bot/human classification, 24-hour summaries, and one-click IP blocking.
- Pro Geo-Fence and trusted-location controls.
- Pro scheduled vulnerability scans and detailed CVE and fix-version information.

## Upgrade Logic

The free plan must create a complete “proof moment”: installation, scan, understandable results, and a small successful cleanup. Pro should be positioned as the plan for **scale, completeness, and recurrence** rather than as a ransom demanded at the point of panic.

Recommended upgrade sequence:

1.  User sees verified threats.
2.  User cleans up to three items with backup.
3.  Product shows remaining risk and explains the consequence of incomplete cleanup.
4.  Pro offers unlimited cleanup and ongoing monitoring.
5.  After recovery, retention shifts from emergency cleanup to scheduled protection and visibility.

# 10 Privacy Security and Data Flow Messaging

## Approved Privacy Narrative

Turbo Guard scans website files on the WordPress installation and does not upload those files to a vendor scanning cloud. External connections are limited to documented services and occur when relevant features are used or enabled.[^3]

| **Service**       | **Data sent**                            | **Trigger**                             | **Message requirement**                                                               |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| WordPress.org API | WordPress version and locale             | Integrity check or scan                 | Explain that checksums come from WordPress.org.                                       |
| WPScan            | Plugin or theme slugs and versions       | Manual or scheduled vulnerability scan  | Do not claim the vulnerability scan is completely offline.                            |
| ipapi.co          | Visitor IP address                       | Country-based Geo-Fence enabled         | State that country lookup requires IP geolocation.                                    |
| OpenAI API        | Anonymised threat-type data              | User initiates AI analysis with own key | Do not imply site content is sent. Keep “anonymised” subject to product verification. |
| Google APIs       | OAuth tokens and site URL or search data | User connects GSC and uses cleanup      | Explain that Google authorisation is user-controlled.                                 |

## Language to Use

- “Your website files stay on your server during scanning.”
- “Optional services connect only when you enable or use them.”
- “No Turbo Guard account is required for the free plugin.”
- “Review documented external services before enabling connected features.”

## Language to Avoid

- “Nothing ever leaves your server.”
- “100 percent private.”
- “Offline security.”
- “No third parties.”
- “Zero data sharing.”

| No telemetry                                      | Approved only while technically true | Any future analytics change requires disclosure and document update.           |

## Claims Requiring Validation

- “Negligible firewall overhead.”
- “Fastest path to recovery.”
- “Zero-risk add-on.”
- “Detection is not signature-delayed” if signature updates or releases are not continuously verified.
- “Unlimited sites” where a public FAQ conflicts with internal Pro site-based licences.
- “60 percent off” without a real, established reference price and renewal disclosure.
- Any numerical cost comparison with competitors.
- Any statement that Google Ads access or organic rankings will be restored.
- Any statement that Turbo Guard detects more malware than another plugin.

## Prohibited Claims

- 100 percent secure.
- Guaranteed malware removal.
- Guaranteed no reinfection.
- Guaranteed no performance impact.
- Guaranteed ranking recovery.
- The only WordPress security plugin.
- Replaces a security professional in every incident.
- Certified, audited, enterprise-grade, or compliant without the named evidence.

# 35 Master Strategic Recommendation

Turbo Guard should enter the market through the urgent recovery problem, not through the broad promise of “more WordPress security features.” The strongest message is that a compromise has two surfaces: the site itself and the public search footprint it leaves behind. Turbo Guard can help users scan and clean the first, address the second through Google Search Console, understand the risk, and strengthen the site afterward.

The brand should therefore be built around **complete, understandable recovery**. “Complete” must remain carefully qualified: Turbo Guard provides a connected self-service workflow, not a guarantee of forensic remediation or ranking restoration. “Understandable” should be visible in product language, onboarding, reports, and campaigns, not confined to the AI feature.

The immediate commercial priority is proof. Improve the WordPress.org presentation, validate cleanup safety and performance, document compatibility and data flows, earn genuine reviews, and publish incident-led content. Once trust improves, Turbo Guard can scale high-intent search acquisition and agency plans from a differentiated position rather than compete in generic security advertising.
