### 3.4 Business Problems Managerium Solves

| **Business Pain**         | **Why It Happens**                                                           | **Managerium Response**                                          |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Scattered Data**        | Departments maintain different records and no single source of truth exists. | Connected database and module-based workflows.                   |
| **Stock Mismatch**        | Sales, purchases, returns, transfers and warehouse records do not match.     | Real-time updates, adjustment, audit and warehouse controls.     |
| **Delayed Reporting**     | Management waits for Excel reports and manual follow-up.                     | Live dashboards and structured module-wise reports.              |
| **Weak Approval Control** | Purchases, expenses and transfers occur without visibility.                  | Workflow approvals and accountable user actions.                 |
| **Finance Disconnect**    | Sales and purchase data are entered into accounts separately.                | Integrated accounting and auto-generated financial transactions. |
| **Retail Counter Errors** | Pricing, discounts, returns and barcode processes vary by staff/outlet.      | POS, permissions and centralized pricing/offer control.          |
| **Growth Chaos**          | More outlets, warehouses or departments increase complexity.                 | Modular scaling and consolidated management visibility.          |
