## 01 Ideal Customer Profile

PeopleDesk primarily serves organisations that need to move from fragmented, manual and people-dependent HR operations to one connected, automated and decision-ready HRM platform.

| **Question**                            | **Answer**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Who Are They?**                       | HR Teams, Admin Teams, Payroll Teams, Finance Teams, Department Heads, CXOs, SMEs, multi-branch companies, factories, retailers, service companies, field-force businesses and large enterprises.                                                |
| **What Do They Currently Think?**       | They believe HR can still be managed through Excel, manual attendance, biometric data, paperwork, scattered employee files and manual payroll. They may also think HRMS is complex, costly or difficult for employees to adopt.                  |
| **What Problem Do They Face?**          | Manual attendance correction, payroll errors, leave approval delays, scattered employee information, shift and roster confusion, field-force attendance control issues and a lack of real-time HR reporting.                                     |
| **What Do We Want Them To Think?**      | PeopleDesk is not just HR software. It is an AI-driven HRM system that connects attendance, leave, payroll, employee records, shift, roster, GPS attendance and HR reports in one platform.                                                      |
| **What Action Do We Want?**             | Book a demo, schedule a call, audit the current HR process and explore how PeopleDesk can automate attendance-to-payroll operations.                                                                                                             |
| **What Proof Will Convince Them?**      | Automated attendance, leave-payroll sync, GPS attendance, shift scheduling, centralised employee records, HR reports, 24/7 support, 99% successful HR and payroll implementation, 30k+ employees managed daily and trust from 50+ organisations. |
| **What Tone Should We Use?**            | Practical, problem-solving, HR-friendly, confidence-building, simple and business-focused. Communication should speak clearly to HR, Finance and CXO-level decision-makers.                                                                      |
| **Which Channels Will Influence Them?** | LinkedIn, Facebook, HR community groups, YouTube explainers, webinars, email campaigns, sales decks, demo sessions, case studies, retargeting ads and direct CXO or HR Head outreach.                                                            |

### 2.4 Logo Guidelines

| **Key Element**                | **Guideline / Brand Information**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Primary & Alternative Logo** | Use the full-colour horizontal logo on white or very light backgrounds. Use a white/reversed wordmark version on Deep Navy backgrounds. | **Clear Space**                | Keep minimum clear space equal to the diameter of one outer circular node around every side of the logo. Recommended standard; final approval should be confirmed against the master logo grid.                                                                                                  |
| **Minimum Size**               | Full logo: recommended minimum 150 px wide for digital and 40 mm for print. Symbol-only: minimum 32 px for digital and 10 mm for print. Test legibility before official approval.                                                                                                                |
| **Correct Usage**              | Preserve proportions, original colours, symbol-wordmark spacing and strong background contrast. Prefer white backgrounds for the full-colour logo.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Incorrect Usage**            | Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, crop, rearrange the people symbol, remove any colour node, add shadows/outlines, use on busy backgrounds or recreate the wordmark by typing.                                                                                                                   |

### Competitive Positioning Principles

- Do not compete only on the number of features. Compete on connected workflows, adoption, implementation reliability and measurable HR outcomes.

- Use attendance-to-payroll accuracy, employee self-service, mobile/GPS attendance, performance, reports and support as the core comparison pillars.

- Avoid attacking competitors directly. Show the operational difference through use cases, dashboards, implementation plans and customer proof.

- Create separate comparison sheets for factories, multi-branch companies, field-force businesses and large enterprises because their buying priorities differ.
