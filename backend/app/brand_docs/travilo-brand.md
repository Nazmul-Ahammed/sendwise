## 01 Ideal Customer Profile

Travilo is designed for travel businesses that need a connected platform for booking, ticketing, finance, partner management, traveller records, reporting and support instead of scattered supplier portals, spreadsheets and manual follow-up.

| **Question**                           | **Answer**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Who Are They?**                      | B2B travel agents, OTA partners, travel agencies, corporate travel service providers, ticketing agents, consolidators, travel startups and businesses managing bookings, deposits, payments, travellers and customer support.                                                                |
| **What Do They Currently Think?**      | Flight booking and ticketing can be managed through supplier portals, calls, spreadsheets and separate payment records. Many think an OTA platform is mainly a flight search tool rather than a complete operating system.                                                                   |
| **What Problem Do They Face?**         | Slow booking workflows, scattered traveller information, unclear booking and ticket status, manual deposit handling, limited financial visibility, partial-payment confusion, support delays and dependency on multiple systems.                                                             |
| **What Do We Want Them To Think?**     | Travilo is not just a booking platform. It is a complete B2B OTA operating system that connects search, booking, ticketing, deposits, ledgers, travellers, agents, reporting and support.                                                                                                    |
| **What Action Do We Want?**            | Apply for a B2B Partner Account, complete company and document verification, request a demo, start using the dashboard, manage deposits and track bookings through Travilo.                                                                                                                  |
| **What Proof Will Convince Them?**     | B2B registration and approval, two-factor authentication, real-time multi-supplier search, booking-status visibility, ticket workflows, payment ledger, deposit request, partial payment, traveller management, role-based access, reporting, support ticketing and global API integrations. |
| **What Tone Should We Use?**           | Professional, reliable, travel-business focused, simple, efficient, partner-friendly, control-focused and finance-transparent.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Which Channel Will Influence Them?** | Facebook travel-agent groups, LinkedIn, Google Search, YouTube explainers, travel communities, direct sales outreach, partner webinars, email campaigns, demos, industry events, WhatsApp groups and referral networks.                                                                      |

### B2B Partner Onboarding and Daily Workflow

| **Stage**                  | **Operational Detail**                                                                                          |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1. Application**         | Prospective partner submits owner information, company information, required documents and password.            |
| **2. Verification**        | Admin reviews documents and either approves, rejects or requests correction.                                    |
| **3. Activation**          | Approved partner must also be active; inactive approved accounts receive a support instruction.                 |
| **4. Secure Login**        | Registered email/password, OTP or two-factor authentication, and optional saved-device flow.                    |
| **5. Search & Booking**    | Search one-way, round-trip or multi-city flights; review fare, baggage, refundability and supplier information. |
| **6. Booking Lifecycle**   | Book, hold, confirm, issue and track tickets; manage failed/in-progress cases, void, refund and reissue.        |
| **7. Finance**             | Request deposits, monitor ledger, paid/due amount, partial payment and transaction records.                     |
| **8. Traveller & Company** | Manage traveller profiles, company details, user settings and password/profile information.                     |
| **9. Support**             | Raise support tickets and track issue resolution.                                                               |

### Implementation Journey

- Discovery: map services, markets, agency model, suppliers, GDS/NDC, payment flows, agent hierarchy and reporting needs.

- Scope design: select Admin, B2B, B2C capability, finance, ticketing, visa, package, CRM, reporting and integration modules.

- Commercial and SLA alignment: confirm setup fee, subscription, integrations, search limits, support and uptime terms.

- Brand and configuration: custom domain, logo, fare rules, commissions, credit, user roles, payment settings and company data.

- Integration and migration: connect suppliers/APIs, import agents, travellers, opening balances and relevant operational records.

- Training and pilot: train admin, ticketing, finance, support and B2B partners; validate booking and payment workflows.

- Go-live: launch controlled production, monitor success/failure cases and resolve configuration gaps.

- Continuous optimisation: improve reports, automation, partner adoption, content, integrations and support quality.
